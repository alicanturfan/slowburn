Help with Slowburn, the card game for two.

Contact

A support email address is being set up and will be listed here.

For anything about a charge or a refund, Apple can help you directly at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple, so they are cancelled in your Apple Account, not inside the game.

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Slowburn and tap Cancel Subscription.

If you are subscribed, the same page opens from inside Slowburn: tap the gear on the first screen, then Manage Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

Tap the gear on the first screen of Slowburn and choose Restore Purchases. The same button is on the screen that offers the subscription. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you bought with; a subscription or a Level 3 purchase comes back on any iPhone using that account.

If you paid but a level is still locked, Restore Purchases fixes it in most cases. A purchase that is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy) unlocks by itself once it is approved.

Refunds

Refunds are decided by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

Face ID lock

The lock is optional and off by default. To turn it on, tap the gear on the first screen and switch on Require Face ID (Require Touch ID on an iPhone with a Home button).

  • Slowburn then asks every time it is opened or brought back from the background.
  • With the lock on, its preview in the app switcher is hidden.
  • If Face ID does not recognise you, iOS offers your device passcode instead.
  • Turning the lock off again asks for Face ID or your passcode once more.

The check is done by iOS. Slowburn never sees your face or fingerprint data, and we cannot unlock the app for you. If you are ever locked out, deleting and reinstalling Slowburn turns the lock off; Restore Purchases then brings your purchases back.

Friday reminder

The reminder is a notification created on your own iPhone. Turn it on or off in Slowburn: tap the gear on the first screen, then Friday reminder. It says only: Your night is waiting.

Free and paid levels

Levels 1 and 2 are free. A weekly or yearly subscription unlocks Levels 3 to 5 and Mutual Yes. Level 3 can also be unlocked on its own with a one-time purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you confirm.

Privacy

Slowburn collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
